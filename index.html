01 / The calling

02 / The whole life

It was never ours.
So we steward it.

Four gifts. One foundation.
A way of living, carried into every day.

01 / Faith

His grace.
Our faith.

Grace is a gift before it is a task. We receive it with gratitude, meet Him in worship, and carry the good news into the world.

In practice Worship. Prayer. Discipleship.
02 / Body

His temple.
Our body.

Our strength is entrusted to us. We train, rest, and care for the body so we can serve well through every season of life.

In practice Discipline. Recovery. Longevity.
03 / Provision

His provision.
Our stewardship.

We work with intention and hold the outcome with humility. What we earn can provide for our households and protect the people counting on us.

In practice Meaningful work. Wise preparation.
04 / People

His people.
Our love.

Our families and neighbors are entrusted to our care. We love through presence, patience, and responsibility. Ministry starts at home.

In practice Presence. Service. A faithful home.
Truth & grace

Held in community.
Leading to communion.

People who tell us the truth, call us higher, and meet our shortcomings with grace. We grow together, with Christ at the center.

flows through you.
